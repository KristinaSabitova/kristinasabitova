## 👾 About me

Before I broke into cybersecurity, I worked as a **Diagnostic Imaging and Nuclear Medicine Technician** — building 3D anatomical models for surgical planning in oncology and complex pathology cases. I know what critical infrastructure looks like when lives depend on it. And I know what it means when it fails.

That perspective changed how I think about security. Now I specialize in **offensive security and OSINT**: finding what's exposed before someone else does, automating reconnaissance, and building tools that do the thinking.

Currently leveling up at **Evolve Academy — Master in Cybersecurity & AI**.

> *From reading CT scans to reading attack surfaces. Same eye. Different target.*
